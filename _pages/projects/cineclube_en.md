Silo's Cineclube holds free film screenings in the Serrinha do Alambari Environmental Protection Area, bringing different generations of the community together through films, gatherings, and conversations. The program creates moments of togetherness and learning through audiovisual work, in partnership with local people and organizations, strengthening access to culture.
