Graciela Selaimen is the founder of Instituto Toriba, a nonprofit organization dedicated to expanding civic imagination through futures literacy and the creation of new narratives for social and environmental transformation. A journalist with a master's degree in Communication and Culture from the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro, Graciela works at the intersection of technology, culture, and social justice.

Between 2013 and 2021, she served as a program director at the Ford Foundation, where she led strategies and grantmaking portfolios in Technology and Society, Creativity and Free Expression, and Civic Space. Her trajectory combines strategic thinking, activist practice, and a radical bet on imagination as a political tool.
