Icaro Ferraz Vidal Junior is a researcher at the Institute of Scientific and Technological Communication and Information in Health (ICICT) of the Oswaldo Cruz Foundation (Fiocruz). He holds a PhD in Communication and Culture from the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ) and in History of Art from the Université de Perpignan Via Domitia (France), in joint supervision with the Università degli Studi di Bergamo (Italy). His research investigates the intersections between contemporary art, science, technology, health, and the environment. With experience curating exhibitions and artistic residencies and editing publications, he has worked at the interface between artistic production and scientific knowledge in recent years.
