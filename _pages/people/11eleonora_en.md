Eleonora Fabião is a performer and performance theorist. She creates actions, exhibits, gives talks, teaches, and publishes nationally and internationally. She performs in diverse contexts – galleries, biennials, festivals and, above all, the streets. Things That Need to Be Done (Performa Biennial, New York, 2015) is the title of one work and also a way of describing her practice. She is a Full Professor at UFRJ, where she teaches in the Theatre Directing program and the Graduate Program in Performing Arts at the School of Communication. She holds a PhD and Master's in Performance Studies from New York University and a Master's in Social History of Culture from PUC-Rio. She holds an Honorary Doctorate from the University of Gothenburg. Since 2019, she has been a CNPq researcher.
